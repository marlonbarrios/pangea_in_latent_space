*A project by the Pangea AI Collective, Marlon Barrios Solano & Maria Luisa Angulo*

**Pangea in Latent Space** is a collaborative, transdisciplinary project that transforms institutional and theatrical spaces into dynamic zones of activation, reflection, and creative experimentation. Conceived by the Pangea AI Collective, Marlon Barrios Solano, and Maria Luisa Angulo (Malú), the project offers a living platform where conversations and actions surrounding artificial intelligence, colonization, and the Global South unfold in real time.

Drawing from their personal trajectories as immigrants—Marlon from Venezuela (based in the United States) and Malú from El Salvador (based in Paris)—the artists weave together embodied practices, emergent technologies, and critical curation. Their collaborative research interrogates the entangled dynamics of hegemonic discourse, algorithmic control, and the cultural shaping of bodies, memory, and futures in the Global South.

At the heart of *Pangea in Latent Space* lies a commitment to speculative creation. The project integrates large language models, real-time image generation, and generative coding with poetic material practices to craft unstable performative landscapes. These are not just technological showcases but porous, conceptual terrains—spaces that resist fixed meaning and invite the emergence of hybrid identities and shifting epistemologies.

Working through **situated ethnography**, each instance of the project is locally anchored, responding to the specific histories, geographies, and lived realities of the communities it engages. Personal storytelling converges with techno-political narratives and the migrations of bodies, forms, and ideas, creating resonant site-specific iterations.

## Project Formats

The project unfolds through multiple interconnected formats:

- **Lecture-Performances**: Layering theory, story, and artistic gesture.  
- **Immersive Installations**: Interactive landscapes exploring AI, memory, and agency.  
- **Movement Scores**: Choreographic frameworks for embodied reflection and performance.  
- **Cartographies & Archives**: Visual mappings of AI, culture, power, and resistance.  
- **Workshops**: Participatory formats that facilitate dialogic learning and collaboration.  
- **Global Dialogues**: Connecting venues and artists across hemispheres, amplifying Southern perspectives on AI and coloniality.

---

**Pangea in Latent Space** is both an artwork and a method. It queers conventional formats, unearths post-geographic imaginaries, and cultivates new modes of sense-making. By fusing speculative practices, generative technologies, and decolonial methodologies, it becomes a transformative ecology—one that reimagines how we inhabit AI and how AI, in turn, inhabits us.
